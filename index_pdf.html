Reel Pipeline

You hit record. The machine does the rest — cut, caption-sync, hook card, export. Zero human editor. This is the exact architecture behind the reel you just watched. Steal it.

The 5-stage pipeline

1

Capture — talk, don't edit

Record one continuous take on a phone or DJI Osmo Pocket. No marks, no re-shoots. The raw clip is the only human input. Vertical 1080×1920 for reels, 1080×1350 for feed.

2

Transcribe — word-level timestamps

Run faster-whisper locally (free, no API). It returns every word with a start/end timestamp. This is the spine — captions and cuts both snap to it.

3

Auto-cut — kill the dead air

A script reads the transcript gaps + silence detection (ffmpeg silencedetect) and trims pauses, filler, and false starts. A 47s ramble becomes a tight 30s cut without anyone touching a timeline.

4

Caption + hook — burned in, beat-snapped

Captions are drawn from the Whisper timestamps so they land on the exact word. The on-screen hook card ("THIS REEL EDITED ITSELF") and brand strap are composited in one ffmpeg filter pass.

5

Export — NVENC, one pass

Single render via ffmpeg NVENC (GPU) with libx264 fallback. Out comes a finished, platform-ready vertical. From record to publish-ready in minutes, not a 3am editing session.
